ABOUT BROOKMOOR FARM

A Farm Rooted in the Community

Brookmoor Farm is presented as a family farm in Waterloo, Western Rural, Freetown, Sierra Leone. Our website introduces visitors to the farm, its produce, and the importance of local agriculture.

Agriculture provides food and supports livelihoods in Sierra Leone. By making information about farm products more accessible, Brookmoor Farm aims to connect interested customers with local farming.

Through this website, visitors can explore our products, learn about our farm, plan a visit, and send enquiries using the contact page.
